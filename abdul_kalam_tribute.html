Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

1931 – 2015

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India (2002–2007). Known as the "Missile Man of India" for his pivotal role in India's missile and nuclear programs, Dr. Kalam inspired millions with his humility, vision, and dedication to science and education.

"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."
— Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Notable Achievements

  • Chief architect of India's civilian space and military missile programs.
  • Played a central role in India's Pokhran-II nuclear tests in 1998.
  • Awarded Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honor, in 1997.
  • Authored inspirational books like Wings of Fire, Ignited Minds, and India 2020.
  • President of India from 2002 to 2007, known as the "People's President".

Legacy

Dr. Kalam's life and work continue to inspire generations of Indians and people around the world. His vision for a developed India, his encouragement for youth to pursue science and innovation, and his humble demeanor have left a lasting impact.
